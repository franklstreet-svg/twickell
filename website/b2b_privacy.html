B2B Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 21, 2026 · Effective: May 21, 2026

Orby AI Solutions ("Orbi AI," "we," "us," or "our") provides B2B software services — the AI Website Controller and the AI Receptionist — to business customers. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we handle the data your customers generate when they interact with these services, where that data lives, and what rights you and your customers have.

1 Overview — A Routing Architecture, Not a Storage Architecture

The short version: Your business data — call transcripts, chat messages, captured leads, business profile, learned answers — lives on hardware you control. Our Bridge service routes secure connections between your hardware and the voice and language-model providers that need them, but the Bridge does not store the content of your customers' conversations. The data your customers create when they interact with Orby is yours, on your hardware. Our records of you are limited to billing, account identity, and aggregate health metrics.

Most AI services are multi-tenant clouds: every customer's data is mixed into one big database operated by the vendor. Orby AI Solutions is deliberately different. The architecture is designed so that the conversations your customers have with Orby are stored on hardware you control rather than on a shared cloud database we control. This is a real privacy differentiator and it is the basis for our pitch to medical practices, legal practices, and other regulated industries.

This Privacy Policy applies to the B2B Products only — the AI Website Controller and the AI Receptionist — and to the marketing website at twickell.com as it relates to the B2B Products. It does not apply to the consumer "My Orby" product, which has its own Privacy Policy at /privacy.

2 Information We Collect About You (the Business Owner)

When you purchase or use a B2B Product, we collect and store the following information about you and your business:

  • Account identity: the email address you register, the business name you provide, and the website URL you provide for the Website Controller.
  • Billing information: processed by Stripe on our behalf. We see only Stripe's tokenized customer and subscription identifiers; we do not see, store, or have access to your full payment card information. Stripe's handling of your card details is governed by Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Owner-token authentication: a long-lived random token tied to your account that authenticates your access to the owner dashboard. The token is sent to the email address you register at checkout.
  • API key: a unique key issued at checkout that authenticates requests from the embed code on your website to the Bridge service.
  • Founding Member status: if you are among the first one thousand (1,000) customers per product, we record your Founding Member number and the discounted setup fee you paid.
  • Heartbeat / health metadata: timestamps recording when your service was last seen alive. These records contain NO content of your conversations.
  • Support correspondence: any email you send to franklstreet@yahoo.com regarding your account.

3 Information Your Customers Create

When your customers interact with Orby on your website or by phone, they generate the following data:

  • Chat or call transcripts of their conversation with Orby
  • Contact information they voluntarily provide (name, phone, email)
  • Description of the service they're inquiring about
  • Urgency level inferred from their words
  • Page URL they were on when they started the chat (Website Controller)
  • Caller phone number (Receptionist — automatically captured from caller ID)
  • Voicemail content if they leave a message

All of this data is stored on hardware you control — your owner dashboard, your customer database, your CRM, or whatever destination you've configured. Orby AI Solutions does not maintain a copy of this data in a shared cloud database. Our Bridge service does route the data in transit (between your customer's browser or phone and your hardware), but the Bridge is not designed to persist the content of these communications.

You are the controller of your customers' data in this sense; Orby AI Solutions acts as a processor on your behalf solely to the extent that the Bridge facilitates the routing.

4 What the Bridge Service Does and Does NOT Do

The Orbi Bridge is the coordination service operated by Orby AI Solutions. Its functions are:

  • Routing secure connections between your hardware and the voice / web services that need to reach it
  • Health monitoring via heartbeat pings (recording only "your service was alive at time T," not the content of any conversation)
  • API key issuance and revocation to authenticate your product instances
  • Billing coordination with Stripe
  • Module library — code templates and industry packs (generic, not customer-specific)
  • Self-healing flags — instructions to restart a product instance if it becomes unresponsive

The Bridge does NOT:

  • Persist the body of customer conversations (chat messages or call transcripts) to its own database
  • Profile your customers across multiple businesses
  • Sell, share, or rent any of your customer data to advertisers, brokers, or any third party
  • Use the content of your conversations to train AI models

The Bridge code is auditable. Customers who wish to inspect the Bridge code to verify these claims may request the audit instructions by emailing franklstreet@yahoo.com.

5 Third-Party Services We Use

To deliver the B2B Products, Orby AI Solutions relies on the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy that you and your customers should be aware of.

Twilio. (For the AI Receptionist.) Twilio carries voice calls between callers and your hardware on the dedicated phone number we provision for your business. Twilio is acting as a telecommunications carrier — analogous to your local phone company — and is governed by its own privacy policy and terms of service. Voice audio passes through Twilio's infrastructure in transit; Twilio's retention policies for transient call audio apply.

Language model providers (Groq, HuggingFace, Anthropic). When Orby needs to generate a response, the conversation context is sent to one of these large language model providers in the following preference order: Groq first (fastest and cheapest), HuggingFace second, Anthropic last (highest quality, used as fallback). Each provider processes the request transiently to generate Orby's reply. The providers' retention policies vary; consult each provider's privacy policy for details.

Stripe. Stripe processes your subscription payment and stores your billing details. Stripe is a PCI-DSS Level 1 service provider. Orby AI Solutions never sees your full card number or CVV.

Hugging Face Spaces. Hugging Face hosts the twickell.com Flask application that serves the marketing site and the Bridge endpoints. Hugging Face Spaces operate under Hugging Face's own privacy and security policies.

Microsoft Edge TTS. Orby's spoken voice on the marketing site is synthesized by Microsoft's Edge TTS service. Text Orby speaks is sent to Microsoft for synthesis and returned as audio. Microsoft does not retain the text or audio beyond the transient synthesis request per its published policy.

Cloudflare (optional). Customers who choose to expose their on-premises hardware to the internet via Cloudflare Tunnel are subject to Cloudflare's privacy policy for the routing of that traffic.

6 How We Use the Information We Collect

Orby AI Solutions uses the limited information we collect for the following purposes only:

  • Service delivery — to operate the B2B Products, route connections, issue API keys, monitor health, and bill you.
  • Customer support — to respond to your questions and resolve issues.
  • Account communications — to email you tier-cap warnings, billing notifications, security advisories, material Terms changes, and feature updates.
  • Fraud prevention and security — to detect and prevent unauthorized access, abuse of the service, or misuse of the embed code or phone numbers.
  • Aggregate, anonymized analytics — to understand how the B2B Products are used in aggregate (e.g., how many active customers, average chat volume per tier). Such analytics are anonymized and do not identify individual customers or their customers.

We do NOT use the information we collect to: (a) sell to data brokers, advertisers, or marketers; (b) build profiles of your customers; (c) train AI models on the content of your conversations; or (d) any purpose unrelated to operating the B2B Products and your account.

7 Cookies and Local Storage

The twickell.com marketing website uses standard browser cookies and local storage for the following limited purposes: maintaining your chat session with Orby on the marketing site, preserving your purchase progress through legal acceptance and Stripe checkout, and storing your owner-token authentication for the dashboard. We do not use cross-site tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies that profile visitors.

The embed code that you install on your website also uses browser localStorage on your visitors' devices to maintain their chat session with Orby. This storage is limited to a session ID and the chat history within that session, and is cleared when the visitor refreshes the page. It is not shared across domains.

8 Data Security

Orby AI Solutions implements commercially reasonable security measures designed to protect the limited information we hold:

  • All traffic between your customer's browser/phone and the Bridge is transmitted over TLS (HTTPS or encrypted SIP)
  • API keys are random 24+ character secrets generated using cryptographically secure randomness
  • Owner tokens are 32-character URL-safe random tokens; the token is required to access the dashboard
  • Stripe secrets and Gmail SMTP credentials are stored as encrypted environment variables on the Hugging Face Spaces platform
  • Customer data files on the Bridge filesystem are accessible only to the running service process

No system is perfectly secure. If you become aware of a security issue with the B2B Products, please report it immediately to franklstreet@yahoo.com.

9 Data Retention

Data on your hardware: retained for as long as you keep it. You may delete it at any time.

Bridge-side records of you:

  • Account identity (email, business name, owner token) — retained while your subscription is active and for ninety (90) days after cancellation, then archived in case of post-cancellation support or chargeback inquiries.
  • API keys — revoked at cancellation; record retained for 90 days then archived.
  • Billing records (invoices, payment receipts) — retained for seven (7) years as required by U.S. tax and accounting law.
  • Support correspondence — retained for two (2) years from the date of the last message.
  • Heartbeat logs — retained for 30 days for operational debugging, then deleted.
