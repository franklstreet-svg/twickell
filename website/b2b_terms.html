2 Description of Services

AI Website Controller. The AI Website Controller is a software service that, when installed on your website via the embed code we provide, displays a chat widget to your visitors and uses large language model technology to converse with them on your behalf. Functions include: greeting visitors; answering frequently asked questions sourced from the business profile you maintain in your owner dashboard; capturing leads and contact information; summarizing visitor inquiries and emailing them to the email address registered for your account; routing emergencies to a phone number you designate; and learning from answers you confirm in your owner dashboard so that future responses incorporate your guidance.

AI Receptionist. The AI Receptionist (currently in pre-launch as of the effective date of these Terms) is a software service that, when activated, answers calls placed to a dedicated phone number we provision for your business and uses synthesized voice plus large language model technology to converse with callers on your behalf. Functions include: greeting callers in your brand voice; taking messages with caller name, callback number, and reason for calling; capturing appointment requests for confirmation by you or your staff; routing emergencies to a phone number you designate; and learning from answers you confirm.
