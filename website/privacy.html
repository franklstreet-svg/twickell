Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 15, 2026  ·  Effective: May 15, 2026

At My Orby, privacy is not an afterthought — it is a core design principle. My Orby is built to run locally on your computer, which means the vast majority of your personal data never leaves your device. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, what stays on your machine, and what rights you have.

1 Overview — A Locally-Installed AI

The short version: My Orby is software that runs on your computer. Your conversations, memories, reminders, notes, family information, health data, and other personal content are stored on your device, not on our servers. We receive very limited technical data necessary to operate the service, and we do not sell your information to anyone.

My Orby is designed on a "local-first" architecture. Unlike cloud-based AI services that store all of your data on remote servers, the Software stores your personal information in a database on your own Windows PC. This means that if our servers were ever compromised, your personal conversations and data would not be exposed, because we do not have them.

This Privacy Policy applies to the My Orby software application, the My Orby website (myorby.com), the My Orby bridge server infrastructure, and all optional software modules. It does not govern the practices of third-party services that My Orby integrates with, including Anthropic's API, Stripe's payment processing, or Microsoft's text-to-speech service. Each of those services has its own privacy policy.

By using the Software or visiting our website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. This Privacy Policy is incorporated by reference into our Terms of Service.

2 Information We Collect

We categorize the data associated with My Orby into three groups: data that stays on your device, data that our servers receive, and data handled by third parties on our behalf.

Stays On Your Device — We Never See This

All of the following is stored exclusively on your computer in the My Orby local database. My Orby's servers do not receive, store, or process any of this data:

Your personal conversations with Orby and all conversation history; reminders, recurring tasks, and task notes; calendar events and scheduling information; personal notes and their tags; shopping lists; financial tracking data including income, expenses, and account information; health information including symptoms, medications, and medical notes; family member information and relationship notes; children's school records, grades, and activity tracking; allowance and chore tracking; journal entries and mood logs; habit and fitness tracking data; pet care records; home maintenance records and warranties; vehicle service records; gift ideas and personal notes about contacts; travel plans and trip history; recipe collections; any other personal content you input into the Software.

Technical Data Our Bridge Server Receives

To operate the bridge server infrastructure — which connects your local Software to Anthropic's AI API and delivers software updates — our servers receive a limited set of technical information. This does not include the content of your conversations or your personal data.

Anonymous Instance Identifier: A randomly generated alphanumeric identifier assigned to your installation of the Software. This ID allows the bridge server to authenticate your license. It is not linked to your name, email address, or any personally identifying information beyond what is needed for license validation.

Software Version: The version number of the My Orby software you are running. Used to determine whether updates are available and to diagnose version-specific issues.

Health and Error Pings: Periodic automated messages sent by the Software to indicate that it is functioning normally or to report errors. These pings contain diagnostic codes, error types, and technical metadata. They do not contain your name, conversation content, or personal information.

License Validation Requests: Periodic requests to verify that your license key is valid and active. These requests include your license key and instance identifier.

Account and Payment Data

Email Address: When you create an account or make a purchase, you provide your email address. We use this address to deliver your license key, send service communications, and provide notice of updates to these policies.

Payment Information: All payment processing is handled by Stripe, Inc. When you enter your credit card or payment information, that information goes directly to Stripe. My Orby never receives, stores, or has access to your full credit card number, CVV, or bank account details. My Orby receives from Stripe only a transaction confirmation, your email address, and a Stripe customer identifier for subscription management.

Purchase Records: My Orby maintains records of your purchases, license keys issued, modules purchased, and subscription status. These records are necessary for account management and customer support.

Website and Marketing Data

When you visit myorby.com, standard web server logs may record your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps. If My Orby implements web analytics tools in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe them. At this time, My Orby does not use third-party behavioral advertising, retargeting, or tracking pixels on its website.

3 How the AI Works — Your Conversations and Anthropic

Understanding how the AI conversation feature works is important to understanding your privacy when using Orby.

When you type or speak a message to Orby and Orby generates a response, the following sequence occurs: (1) your message, along with relevant context that Orby needs to answer helpfully (such as information about the current date, your active module context, or relevant facts from your local database that Orby selects to include), is assembled into a prompt by the Software running on your computer; (2) this assembled prompt is transmitted over an encrypted connection through My Orby's bridge server to Anthropic, PBC's API; (3) Anthropic's servers generate a response using the Claude AI model; (4) the response is returned through the bridge server to your Software; and (5) Orby displays and/or speaks the response to you. The conversation exchange is then stored in the local database on your device.

What this means for your privacy: The content of your AI conversations — your messages and Orby's responses — is transmitted to Anthropic's servers for the purpose of generating responses. My Orby does not store the content of these messages on its own servers. However, Anthropic does receive and process your messages. Anthropic's data practices are governed by Anthropic's own privacy policy, available at anthropic.com/privacy. My Orby encourages you to review Anthropic's privacy policy. My Orby is not responsible for Anthropic's data handling practices.

My Orby's bridge server acts as a relay for AI queries. The bridge server receives encrypted AI query packets but is architecturally designed not to log or store the content of individual AI queries. However, aggregate and anonymized usage statistics — such as the total number of queries processed per time period — may be retained for infrastructure planning purposes.

If you are concerned about sensitive information being transmitted to Anthropic, you should be aware that: (a) information you choose to share with Orby in conversation may be included in the context sent to Anthropic; (b) information stored in your local database is only sent to Anthropic when Orby determines it is relevant to your query and includes it in the context — this selection happens locally on your device; (c) you can control what you share with Orby by choosing what information you input into the Software.

My Orby does not share your personal information with advertisers, data brokers, marketing companies, or analytics firms for commercial purposes.

My Orby does not build behavioral profiles of you for the purpose of targeted advertising, credit scoring, insurance rating, employment screening, or any other commercial profiling purpose.

My Orby does not access, read, scan, or transmit your local files, documents, photos, or other data stored on your computer beyond what the Software explicitly stores in its own local database with your knowledge.

My Orby does not monitor your other applications, websites you visit, or activities outside the My Orby Software.

The locally-installed design of My Orby is not merely a feature — it is the foundational reason why we collect so little data. We have deliberately chosen an architecture that keeps your personal life on your machine. We believe that your conversations with your AI companion are private and should remain so.

8 European Users — GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area ("EEA"), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") and applicable local data protection laws may apply to My Orby's processing of your personal data.

Our Legal Bases for Processing

My Orby processes minimal personal data about European users. Where we do process personal data, we rely on the following legal bases under the GDPR:

  • Contract Performance (Article 6(1)(b)): Processing your email address, license key, and account information is necessary to perform the contract we have with you — delivering the Software, managing your subscription, and providing customer support.
  • Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f)): Processing anonymized technical data (health pings, error reports, instance identifiers) is necessary for My Orby's legitimate interests in operating, maintaining, and improving the Software, provided those interests are not overridden by your fundamental rights.
  • Legal Obligation (Article 6(1)(c)): We may process personal data where required by applicable law.

Your GDPR Rights

  • Right of Access (Article 15): You have the right to obtain confirmation of whether My Orby processes personal data about you and, if so, to access that data and receive a copy.
  • Right to Rectification (Article 16): You have the right to have inaccurate personal data corrected and incomplete personal data completed.
  • Right to Erasure (Article 17): You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data in certain circumstances, such as where the data is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (Article 18): You have the right to request that My Orby restrict the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability (Article 20): Where processing is based on your consent or contract performance, you have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format and to transmit it to another controller.
  • Right to Object (Article 21): You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation.

International Data Transfers

My Orby is based in the United States. When you use the Software and your queries are processed through Anthropic's API, your data is transferred to and processed in the United States. My Orby relies on standard contractual clauses and other appropriate transfer mechanisms to the extent required by applicable law for any transfers of personal data from the EEA to the United States.

To exercise your GDPR rights, please contact us at privacy@myorby.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

9 Data Security

Local Data Security. Data stored on your computer in the My Orby local database is subject to the same security as your device itself. The security of your locally-stored data depends on your device's security — including your operating system's built-in protections, your user account password, disk encryption (such as BitLocker on Windows), and your personal security practices. My Orby strongly recommends enabling full-disk encryption on your computer and using a strong account password. My Orby is not responsible for the security of your locally-stored data.

Bridge Server Security. Communications between the Software and the My Orby bridge server are encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS). My Orby uses industry-standard security measures to protect its bridge server infrastructure from unauthorized access, including firewalls, access controls, and security monitoring.

AI Query Encryption. AI queries transmitted from your Software through the bridge server to Anthropic's API are transmitted over encrypted connections.

No System Is Perfectly Secure. While My Orby takes security seriously and implements reasonable technical and organizational measures, no security system is impenetrable and My Orby cannot guarantee the absolute security of any data. My Orby is not responsible for any breach of security caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including attacks by sophisticated threat actors, zero-day vulnerabilities, or failures of third-party infrastructure.

Backup Your Data. Because your My Orby data is stored locally on your computer, a hard drive failure, system crash, theft, or loss of your device could result in permanent loss of all data stored by the Software. My Orby strongly recommends maintaining regular backups of your data to an external drive, cloud backup service, or other backup solution. My Orby is not responsible for data loss resulting from device failure or loss.

Data Breach Notification. In the event of a data breach that My Orby discovers and that affects personal data under My Orby's control, My Orby will notify affected users and applicable regulatory authorities as required by applicable law.

14 AI Receptionist & AI Website Controller — Data Routing Addendum

The short version (B2B products): When you buy the AI Receptionist or AI Website Controller, your business data — call transcripts, chat messages, customer leads, business profile, and learned answers — lives on hardware you control. Our Bridge service routes secure connections between your hardware and the voice/web services that need them, but it does not store the content of your customers' conversations.

14.1 What lives on YOUR hardware

Your AI Receptionist or AI Website Controller stores the following data on hardware you control (your office server, your own cloud VPS, or your own computer):

  • Your business profile (name, hours, services, pricing, owner info, FAQs)
  • All call transcripts and chat conversation history
  • Leads and contact information your AI captures
  • Owner-confirmed answers (your AI's learned knowledge base)
  • Notifications and message-taking records

You can wipe this data at any time. You can move it to different hardware. You hold the keys.

14.2 What our Bridge service DOES handle

The Orbi Bridge is our coordination service. It runs on Orbi AI infrastructure and performs these functions:

  • Routing connections between your hardware and the voice/web services that need to reach it (for example, between Twilio's voice service and the brain on your hardware)
  • Health monitoring via heartbeat pings — recording only "your service was alive at this time," never the content of the conversations
  • API key issuance to authenticate your products
  • Billing coordination with Stripe (we never see your card numbers — those go to Stripe directly)
  • Module library — code and configuration templates for industry-specific features

The Bridge passes through data in transit. It does not persist the body of customer conversations, chat messages, or call transcripts to its own database.

14.3 Third-party services involved

To deliver voice and chat capabilities, your AI uses these third-party services:

  • Twilio (for AI Receptionist) — carries voice calls between callers and your hardware. Twilio is the phone carrier; their privacy policy governs voice transit, the same way any phone carrier would. You hold your own Twilio account and pay Twilio directly.
  • Groq, HuggingFace, Anthropic — large language model providers used (in that order of preference) when your AI generates a response. These providers process the conversation transiently to generate the AI's reply. They do not retain conversation content beyond their stated retention windows (see each provider's policy).
  • Stripe — payment processing for your subscription. Stripe sees your billing details; we do not.
  • Render — hosts our Bridge service. Render's infrastructure runs the routing code; it does not have direct access to your stored business data.

14.4 HIPAA-friendly architecture

Because your business data does not live in a shared multi-tenant cloud database we control, the AI Receptionist and AI Website Controller architecture is suitable for many regulated industries — including medical, legal, and financial offices — that require strong data residency and isolation guarantees. We do not currently sign HIPAA Business Associate Agreements; if your use case requires one, contact us so we can discuss a custom arrangement.

14.5 Your right to audit

The Orbi Bridge router code is open to inspection by customers. If you want to confirm that our service does what we describe, request the audit instructions by emailing franklstreet@yahoo.com.

14.6 Data deletion

To delete your B2B AI's data, you have two paths:

  • Delete from your hardware: simply remove the brain installation from your server. All conversation history, leads, and learned answers are gone.
  • Cancel your subscription: we will revoke your API keys, deactivate your Bridge instance entries, and email you a confirmation. Your data on your hardware remains yours to do with as you wish.

This Section 14 addendum specifically governs the AI Receptionist and AI Website Controller B2B products. Sections 1–13 above continue to govern the consumer My Orby product and the marketing website at twickell.com.
