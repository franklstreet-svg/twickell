2 One-Time Setup Fee

Each B2B Product subscription includes a one-time setup fee of $299, which is reduced to $149.50 for Founding Members (first one thousand paying customers per product). The setup fee is non-refundable except where required by law.

The setup fee compensates Orby AI Solutions for the immediate work of provisioning your account, including: generating your unique API key; issuing your long-lived owner token; running our universal scraper on the website URL you provide to pre-populate your business profile; configuring the appropriate industry pack for your business type; generating and emailing your embed code for the Website Controller, or arranging your dedicated phone number for the Receptionist; and providing onboarding email support. This work is performed at the moment of checkout, regardless of how long you ultimately keep the subscription, which is why the fee is non-refundable. If you cancel within an hour of paying, the setup work has still been done.

If you believe your local consumer-protection law entitles you to a refund of the setup fee (for example, certain EU consumer-rights regimes provide cooling-off periods that may apply), please email franklstreet@yahoo.com with your jurisdiction and a description of the basis for the refund request, and we will respond.

3 Monthly Subscription — 7-Day Pro-Rated Refund

If you cancel within the first seven (7) days of any monthly billing cycle, we will refund a pro-rated portion of that month's subscription fee for the unused days. The refund will be processed through Stripe to your original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days depending on your card issuer.

Example: Your subscription renews on the 1st of the month and you cancel on the 4th. Three days of the cycle have been used; 27 unused days will be refunded at 27/30 × your monthly fee. The one-time setup fee, if originally charged at the start of this cycle, is not refunded.

After day seven of a billing cycle, monthly fees are non-refundable for that cycle. The subscription will not renew once canceled; you continue to have full access to the B2B Product for the remainder of the cycle.

4 How to Cancel

You can cancel your subscription using any of the following methods. All are equally effective.

  • Owner dashboard: log in at twickell.com/dashboard?token=<your token> and use the "Cancel subscription" button. The button is in your account settings panel.
  • Email: send a message to franklstreet@yahoo.com from the email address registered for your account, with the subject line "Cancel Subscription." Include the business name so we can identify the account. We will confirm cancellation within 24 hours.
  • Stripe customer portal: if you have access to the Stripe customer portal link sent in your welcome email, you may cancel directly from there. The Bridge will be notified by the next billing cycle.

Cancellation is immediate upon receipt. Your subscription will continue to provide service through the end of the current billing cycle, after which it will not renew.

5 What Happens to Your Data on Cancellation

Data on your hardware: remains yours. Cancellation does not delete anything that lives on hardware you control — your business profile, learned answers, leads, call transcripts, and chat history all stay where they are. You may delete or retain them at your discretion.

API key: revoked at cancellation. The embed code on your website will stop responding; you should remove the embed snippet from your site to avoid showing visitors a dead chat bubble.

Dedicated phone number (Receptionist): released at cancellation. Forwarded calls will no longer be answered. You should update any business listings, voicemail messages, or printed materials that referred to this number.

Bridge records: your account identity record is marked canceled and archived for 90 days in case of post-cancellation support or chargeback inquiries. After 90 days the active record is deleted; billing records are retained per the seven-year tax-law requirement noted in our Privacy Policy.

Founding Member status: tied to the original active subscription. If you cancel and later re-subscribe, you will not retain Founding Member status (and you will pay the full setup fee on the new subscription), even if Founding Member capacity still exists.

6 Service-Interruption Credits

Orby AI Solutions uses commercially reasonable efforts to keep the B2B Products available 24/7. We do not guarantee uninterrupted service.

If the B2B Products are unavailable for more than twenty-four (24) consecutive hours in a calendar month due to causes within Orby AI Solutions's reasonable control, you may request a pro-rated service credit on your next bill. To request a credit, email franklstreet@yahoo.com with the subject line "Service Credit Request" and include the approximate dates and times of the outage as you experienced it.

Service credits are NOT available for downtime caused by:

  • Upstream third-party provider outages (Hugging Face, Stripe, Microsoft Azure, Twilio, Groq, Anthropic, or any language-model provider) — these are outside our control
  • Your own internet connection or hardware
  • Your local power outage or your own infrastructure failure
  • Scheduled maintenance windows we have announced in advance
  • Force majeure events (acts of God, natural disasters, government action, etc.)
  • Suspension under Section 5 of the Terms of Service due to suspected misuse

Service credits are issued as bill reductions on a future invoice. They are not redeemable for cash.

7 Tier Changes (Upgrades and Downgrades)

Upgrade. Tier upgrades take effect immediately. The price difference is pro-rated for the remainder of the current billing cycle and charged on your next Stripe invoice. For example, upgrading from Starter ($99/mo) to Growth ($199/mo) on day 15 of a 30-day cycle charges (15/30) × ($199 − $99) = $50 on the next invoice; the following month bills at the full $199.

Downgrade. Tier downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. Orby AI Solutions does not credit the difference between tiers retroactively, because the higher-tier capacity was available to you for the full current cycle. You retain access to the higher tier through the end of the current cycle.

8 Chargebacks and Disputes

If you believe a charge on your account is incorrect, please contact us at franklstreet@yahoo.com BEFORE initiating a chargeback with your card issuer. Most billing issues can be resolved within 1–3 business days through direct communication.

Initiating a chargeback on a charge that complied with this policy and the Terms of Service may result in immediate suspension of your account pending resolution. Orby AI Solutions reserves the right to provide the relevant transaction records, terms of service, and cancellation logs to the card network to defend against chargebacks that we believe to be unwarranted.

9 Founding Member Discount and This Policy

The Founding Member 50% discount on the one-time setup fee (reducing $299 to $149.50) does not affect any of the refund provisions in this policy. The discounted setup fee remains non-refundable on the same basis as the full setup fee, monthly subscription pricing is the same as for non-founders, and the seven-day pro-rated refund window applies identically. Founding Member status is recorded in your owner dashboard and serves as a permanent record of your early-customer status; it is not a refundable benefit.
