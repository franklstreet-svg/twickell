How it works

From "I want to buy" to "Orby is live on my site" in under 24 hours.

You buy a tier that matches your traffic.

Pay with Stripe like any other subscription. We email you within minutes with your owner dashboard link and your embed code (for the Website Controller) or your Twilio setup instructions (for the Receptionist).

Orby scans your website to learn the basics.

Our scraper pulls your business name, services, hours, contact info, owner name, and service area from your existing site. You don't need to fill anything out manually — but you'll review it before going live.

You confirm her business profile in the dashboard.

Edit anything the scraper missed or got wrong. Add specifics — your appointment rules, your pricing, your emergency triage flow, what to say when a caller asks about something you don't do. Takes about 20 minutes.

You paste one line of code (Website Controller) or forward your phones (Receptionist).

For chat: copy the <script> tag we emailed you and paste it into your website's footer. Reload — Orby appears in the bottom-right corner. For phone: forward your business line to the Twilio number we set up. Orby starts answering on the very next call.

She learns every time she's stumped.

Whenever a caller or visitor asks something she doesn't know, she captures the question and notifies you. You answer once in your dashboard. From that moment, every future customer with the same question gets your answer instantly. Her knowledge compounds — month two is better than month one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Real questions from real prospects. Missing something? Just ask Orby.

Will Orby sound robotic?

No. Orby uses natural-sounding voices that callers regularly mistake for a human receptionist. She handles interruptions, knows when to slow down for a phone number, and uses warm conversational phrasing — not telephone-tree menus.

For the Receptionist, you can pick from multiple voice options (Polly Standard included free; premium neural voices available as an upgrade).

What if Orby doesn't know how to answer something?

She'll say something honest like "That's a great question — I'm going to make sure the right person here gets back to you on that. What's the best number to reach you at?" She captures the question and notifies you immediately.

You answer once in your dashboard. The next caller or visitor with the same question gets your answer instantly. Her knowledge base grows with you.

How long does setup take?

For the Website Controller: you'll have your embed code in your inbox within minutes of buying. Pasting it on your site is a 5-minute job (longer if your developer is asleep). Orby starts working the moment the page reloads.

For the Receptionist: setup is under 24 hours from purchase. We confirm your business profile with you, set up the Twilio webhook, and walk you through forwarding your business line.

Does Orby work in languages other than English?

Yes. She handles Spanish, French, and a long list of others automatically — she'll respond in whatever language the caller or visitor uses. If you want her to default to a specific language for greetings, you can set that in your profile.

What happens during a tier cap?

We email you at 80% of your monthly cap and again at 100%. If you go over, Orby keeps working — we don't cut you off in the middle of a call or chat. But we'll suggest the next tier up so you're not over-paying for usage you keep exceeding.

You can change tiers any time from your dashboard.

Why do I need my own Twilio account for the Receptionist?

Twilio is the phone carrier — like Verizon or AT&T, but for software. The reason you bring your own Twilio account is so the phone bill stays in your name (and your phone numbers stay portable if you ever leave us). You pay Twilio at their published per-minute rates, which is usually $15-$300/month depending on call volume. We help you set it up — it's a 10-minute process.

Is this HIPAA-friendly for medical or legal use?

The architecture is — because your patient or client data lives on hardware you control, never in a shared cloud database. We don't currently sign HIPAA Business Associate Agreements off-the-shelf. If your practice requires one, contact us and we'll arrange a custom setup.

What if Orby messes something up?

It can happen. She has hard limits built in (she'll never give legal advice, medical advice, or financial advice — she'll always defer those to you). For other mistakes, every conversation is visible in your dashboard. You can correct what she said, and she'll learn from your correction so the same mistake doesn't happen twice.

If something is wrong, email franklstreet@yahoo.com — we read every message.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from your dashboard or by emailing us. No notice period. No cancellation fees. We'll deactivate your products, you keep your data (it was on your hardware anyway), and we won't bill you again.

What is "founding member pricing"?

Founding Member pricing is a launch offer for our first 1,000 paying customers per product. As a Founding Member, you pay $149.50 for the one-time setup fee instead of the regular $299 — a 50% savings. Your monthly subscription rate is the same as everyone else's (Starter $99/mo, Growth $199/mo, Pro $349/mo); the discount applies only to the setup fee.

The discount is applied automatically at checkout — you don't need a code. Your Founding Member number (e.g., "Founding Member #14 of 1,000") appears in your owner dashboard so you have a record of it. Once we hit 1,000 founders on a given product, the setup fee returns to $299 for new customers, but everyone who got in early keeps their original deal.
